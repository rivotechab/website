What we do

We place consultants in four areas. Tell us the role you need to fill.

  • Software development

    Developers who build and maintain the systems your business runs on, from APIs and services to the screens your users see.

    • Backend developer
    • Frontend developer
    • Fullstack developer

  • Cloud, infrastructure & DevOps

    Engineers who set up and run your cloud platforms, automate builds and releases, and keep environments stable.

    • Cloud engineer
    • Infrastructure engineer
    • DevOps engineer

  • Data & AI

    Engineers and scientists who build data pipelines, find answers in your data and put language models to work in your products.

    • Data engineer
    • Data scientist
    • AI and LLM engineer

  • Solution architecture

    Architects who design how your systems fit together and guide teams from the first sketch to production.

    • Solution architect

How we work

  1. Send us the role

    Stack, seniority, start date, location and how long you need someone. A short description is enough.

  2. Get matching profiles

    We send CVs of consultants who fit, with availability and rate. We only present people who have agreed to it.

  3. Interview and start

    You meet the consultant and decide. The assignment runs on a contract with Rivo Tech AB, directly or through your broker's framework agreement.

Send the role to info@rivotech.se

Careers

We look for developers, engineers and architects who want client assignments in Sweden. Experienced consultants and recent graduates are both welcome, including international graduates who studied here.

  1. Send your CV

    Email it to careers@rivotech.se, in English or Swedish. PDF works best.

  2. Talk to us

    If we see a role that fits, we set up a short call about your experience and what you are looking for.

  3. Get presented, with your OK

    We never share your CV with a client without asking you first.

How we handle your CV

Rivo Tech AB (org. nr 559537-2755) is responsible for the information you send us. We use it only to assess you for roles at Rivo or with our clients, and we delete it after 12 months. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing careers@rivotech.se. You can also contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) if you have concerns.
